Brag Plan: SearchTern
What is this app?

SearchTern is an internship search and application tracker built for students.

It brings thousands of internships into one searchable feed and gives students one place to manage the entire application pipeline:

Find → Save → Apply → Interview → Offer → Rejected

Instead of bouncing between job boards, spreadsheets, notes, and browser tabs, SearchTern puts the search and tracking process together.

The Angle

The modern internship hunt is scattered everywhere.

You're checking job boards, company career pages, spreadsheets, bookmarks, Discord messages, GitHub lists, and random links people send you.

SearchTern turns that mess into one system.

The video should tell a simple story:

The internship hunt is chaotic. SearchTern makes it manageable.

No corporate language.
No exaggerated AI claims.
No complicated explanation of the backend.

Just show the problem → show SearchTern → show the workflow.

The product itself is the advertisement.
